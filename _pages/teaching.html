--- layout: faculty title: Teaching & Mentoring permalink: /teaching/ eyebrow: EDUCATION lede: "Connecting mathematical foundations with systems practice, critical experimentation, and responsible applications." --- ## Teaching approach I emphasize the relationship between mathematical models, implementable systems, and the evidence needed to decide whether an AI method works. Students should be able to derive central ideas, build reproducible experiments, and explain the limitations of their conclusions. ## Courses and curriculum development
