## CS 691 — Special Topics: Neurosymbolic Artificial Intelligence

**Spring 2026 · Department of Computer Science · The University of Alabama**

This graduate special-topics course explored the integration of symbolic reasoning and contemporary machine learning, with particular emphasis on large language models and knowledge-grounded AI systems.

### Topics

<div class="mini-grid">
  <div class="info-card"><p class="kicker">01 · FOUNDATIONS</p><h3>Symbolic Knowledge Representation</h3><p>First-order logic (FOL), predicates, quantifiers, inference, ontologies, knowledge graphs, and formal representation of domain knowledge.</p></div>
  <div class="info-card"><p class="kicker">02 · INTEGRATION</p><h3>Neural–Symbolic Architectures</h3><p>Hybrid neural and symbolic approaches, knowledge-infused learning, constraint-guided reasoning, and integration of structured knowledge with learned representations.</p></div>
  <div class="info-card"><p class="kicker">03 · LANGUAGE MODELS</p><h3>LLMs and Knowledge-Grounded Reasoning</h3><p>Combining large language models with knowledge graphs, logical constraints, retrieval, symbolic planning, and evidence-based reasoning.</p></div>
  <div class="info-card"><p class="kicker">04 · APPLICATIONS</p><h3>Trustworthy and Applied Neurosymbolic AI</h3><p>Explainability, causal and process-aware reasoning, evaluation of hybrid systems, and applications in healthcare and decision support.</p></div>
</div>
