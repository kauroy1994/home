## Our mission

The **MIND Lab** at The University of Alabama pursues research at the intersection of machine learning, knowledge representation, causal models, and decision-making. Our goal is to build AI that can combine the flexibility of modern foundation models with the discipline of explicit knowledge, processes, and verifiable evidence.

## What we work on

Our active themes include **neurosymbolic learning**, **causal reasoning**, **agentic AI and verification**, **process-grounded question answering**, and **trustworthy AI for healthcare and industrial processes**. Our projects span theory, methods, systems, and applied evaluation.

For an overview of research directions, visit [Research]({{ '/research/' | relative_url }}); for peer-reviewed outputs and preprints, visit [Publications]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}).
