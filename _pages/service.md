## Scholarly engagement

Academic service is an important part of my research and teaching mission. My interests in research-community activities include **peer review, program development, tutorials, interdisciplinary panels, and scholarly exchanges on trustworthy AI**.

The site currently prioritizes activities for which public evidence is available. Detailed lists of review assignments, confidential panel work, or proposed-but-unaccepted events are intentionally not published as completed service.
