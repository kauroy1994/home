ACADEMIC PROFILE
Researcher and faculty member developing neurosymbolic artificial intelligence that integrates learned representations with
explicit declarative, procedural, and causal knowledge for reliable reasoning, sequential decision-making, and interpretable
systems. Research spans knowledge-infused learning, cognitive architectures, large language models, healthcare AI,
spatiotemporal understanding, and trustworthy AI.

RESEARCH SPECIALIZATIONS
Neurosymbolic AI • Knowledge and process-grounded reasoning • Explainability and AI safety • Sequential decision-making
and reinforcement learning • Cognitive architectures and model-based agents • Knowledge graphs • Healthcare and social-
good AI • Retrieval, evaluation, and multimodal reasoning
